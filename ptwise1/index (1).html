How PitWise works
  • Each analysis runs 2,000 simulated races. Random Safety Car periods, weather changes and rival pit calls happen in each race.
  • All four options face the same random events in each race, so the comparison is fair.
  • The option that loses the least time in the most races gets the recommendation.
  • Worn tyres cost more time each lap. Wear below about 40% costs almost nothing.
  • A pit stop costs 22 seconds, or 11 seconds under a Safety Car.
  • Dry tyres in the rain lose 1.2 seconds per lap. Intermediate tyres lose 0.1 seconds per lap.
  • A rival pit call adds 2 seconds to staying out, as a rough guess for losing track position.
  • These are assumptions chosen for this prototype, not measured Formula 1 data.

How to use PitWise

  1. Log in. Enter your driver name (and team, optional). Your name appears at the top right.
  2. Race dashboard. See the lap, position, tyre wear, weather, Safety Car and rival status at a glance.
  3. Race conditions. Set the current lap, your position, tyre wear, weather, Safety Car and rival pit call.
  4. Analyse race. Press the button. PitWise runs 2,000 simulated races and opens the strategy page.
  5. Strategy page. Read the recommendation and the win chances. A higher win chance means that option lost the least time in more races.
  6. Log out. Use the Log out button at the top right when you finish.
